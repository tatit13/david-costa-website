ABOUT DAVID

A hands-on maritime professional with a safety-first mindset.

I am a calm, responsible and proactive professional with a long-standing background in maritime rescue, water safety and nautical activities. My work has involved operating rescue boats, coordinating lifesaving services and teaching kitesurfing to adults and children.

I enjoy practical work, take responsibility seriously and am motivated to continue developing my career at sea.
